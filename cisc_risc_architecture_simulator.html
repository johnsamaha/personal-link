01 / ISA DESIGNInstruction count ≠ execution time

CISC can express more work in one instruction; RISC often expresses it as several simpler instructions. You must also consider how those instructions are implemented.

02 / IMPLEMENTATIONPipelines versus micro-steps

The RISC teaching core overlaps instructions across five stages. The CISC teaching core uses sequential internal steps. The difference here is a model choice, not a universal law of the two ISAs.

03 / REAL-WORLD SYSTEMSModern processors are more complex

Real x86 CPUs may translate instructions into micro-ops, pipeline, and execute them out of order. Actual speed also depends on clock rate, caches, branch behavior, compiler, and workload.
